00–10 seconds
Meet CrewSync AI
CrewSync helps student clubs and volunteer organizers bring people together. It keeps event plans, shifts, tasks, and communication in one workspace.

10–20 seconds
Start with your people
First, add your volunteers and the hours they can help. Then enter the tasks, shift times, and number of people needed.

20–30 seconds
Describe the event
With Luminal connected, describe your event in simple English. The AI suggests shifts, a checklist, and an announcement. Review everything before approving.

30–40 seconds
Build a clear schedule
CrewSync assigns available volunteers and checks coverage, overlapping shifts, and workload limits. This fictional football event needs nine assignments across three shifts.

40–50 seconds
Handle last-minute changes
If someone cancels, preview a new schedule before applying it. CrewSync can use an available replacement and clearly shows any staffing gap.

50–60 seconds
Keep the crew in sync
Draft reminders, track completed tasks, and save your event to reopen later. You stay in control. CrewSync: you lead, we coordinate.
