# CrewSync AI · 90-second demonstration

Use synthetic volunteer data. Configure Luminal beforehand and verify one request. If it is unavailable, openly describe the fixture/template demonstration as demo mode.

**0:00–0:15 · Problem**
“Volunteer coordinators juggle availability, staffing gaps and last-minute changes. CrewSync gives them one workspace to prepare a plan and keep the team aligned.”

**0:15–0:35 · Describe and approve**
Enter: “Campus Giving Day tomorrow, 10 AM to 4 PM at the HUB entrance. We need check-in, a fundraising booth and cleanup. Each shift needs two people. Prepare a checklist and announcement.”
Click Draft my event plan. Review the proposed date, location, shifts and checklist. Approve & create event. Show availability-based assignments and the live source label. Explain that proposed defaults must be reviewed.

**0:35–0:50 · Coordinate**
Assign a checklist task to a volunteer, set its due date and mark it complete. Click Update checklist. Show the progress bar.

**0:50–1:10 · Adapt**
Preview a cancellation. Show revised assignments and any remaining gaps. Approve it. Explain that the affected volunteer's checklist responsibilities become Unassigned, and no messages are sent automatically. Generate a coordinator briefing or help-request drafts.

**1:10–1:25 · Persist**
Save current event. Start a new sample event and reopen the saved one. Show the restored tasks and roster. If MongoDB is verified, show its events collection; otherwise clearly identify local storage.

**1:25–1:30 · Close**
“You lead. We coordinate. AI proposes the plan and communication; coordinators approve changes, and Python checks scheduling constraints.”
